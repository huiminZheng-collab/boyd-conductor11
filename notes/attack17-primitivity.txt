\begin{proposition}\label{prop:delta-primitive}
The cycle $\delta=\{0,\tfrac{3}{11}\}-\{0,\tfrac{8}{11}\}$ generates
$H_1(X_1(11),\mathbf{Z})^{-}\cong\mathbf{Z}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
We compute the integral homology of $X_1(11)$ by Manin symbols for the group
$\pm\Gamma_1(11)$, which has index~$60$ in $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbf{Z})$
(\texttt{code/siegel\_anchor\_step13.py}; all linear algebra is over
$\mathbf{Z}$). Cosets $\pm\Gamma_1(11)\backslash\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbf{Z})$
are parametrized by nonzero pairs $(c,d)\in(\mathbf{Z}/11)^2$ modulo $\pm1$
(the bottom row of a matrix), giving $60$ Manin symbols $x_i=\{g_i\infty,g_i0\}$
subject to $x+xS=0$ and $x+xR+xR^2=0$ with $S=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\
1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, $R=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\1&1
\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. The boundary map $\partial(x_i)=[g_i0]-[g_i\infty]$
lands in the divisor group of the $10$ cusps
(double cosets $\pm\Gamma_1(11)\backslash\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbf{Z})/\langle
T\rangle$). A Smith normal form computation gives
$H_1=\ker\partial\cong\mathbf{Z}^2$, torsion-free, with an explicit integral
basis. Complex conjugation $c\colon\tau\mapsto-\bar\tau$ acts on bottom rows
by $(c,d)\mapsto(-c,d)$ and induces $C=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0
\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ on $H_1$; hence
$H_1^{-}=\ker(C+I)=\mathbf{Z}\cdot(-1,1)$ has rank one. The
continued-fraction expansion expresses $\delta$ as the signed sum of the
seven Manin symbols $[[1,0],[3,1]]$, $-[[1,1],[3,4]]$, $[[3,1],[11,4]]$,
$-[[1,0],[1,1]]$, $[[1,2],[1,3]]$, $-[[3,2],[4,3]]$, $[[3,8],[4,11]]$;
its boundary telescopes to $[3/11]-[8/11]=0$ since $3/11$ and $8/11$ define
the same $\pm\Gamma_1(11)$-cusp, so $\delta$ is closed. In the integral
basis of $H_1$ the class of $\delta$ has coordinates $(1,-1)$: it satisfies
$C(1,-1)^t=-(1,-1)^t$ and $\gcd(1,-1)=1$, i.e.\ $\delta=-1\cdot(-1,1)$ is
\emph{exactly} $\pm1$ times a generator of $H_1^{-}$. Equivalently, $\delta$
has intersection number $\pm1$ with an integral class.
\end{proof}
