# Response to the third referee report

We thank the referee for the precise statement of the remaining blocker. The
factor of two has now been located **at source level**: it is the index of the
anti-invariant *subgroup* in the coinvariant *quotient* — item 5 of the
referee's list ("the homology generator"). The manuscript no longer asserts
`c=2` anywhere; there is a single normalization (`c=1`) throughout, and the
apparent contradiction is resolved by distinguishing the two standard meanings
of "$H_1(E,\mathbb Z)^-$". All revisions below are in the revision tagged
`rev3`.

## 1. Where the factor 2 lives

The regulator pairing of Lalín–Ramamonjisoa (their Def. 3) is

\[
\gamma \longmapsto \int_\gamma \eta(f,g).
\]

Their Remark 4 observes that this pairing **vanishes on
$H_1(E,\mathbb Z)^+$**: if $c(\gamma)=\gamma$ then
$\int_\gamma\eta=\int_{c(\gamma)}\eta=\int_\gamma c^*\eta=-\int_\gamma\eta$
since $c^*\eta=-\eta$. The pairing therefore descends to the **coinvariant
quotient** $H_1/H_1^+$, and Bloch's theorem (their Thm. 6) evaluates it on a
generator of *that* group, with factor 1.

The two meanings of "anti-invariant generator" differ exactly when $\Delta<0$:

- $\Delta>0$ (conductor 17): $c_*: a\mapsto a,\ b\mapsto -b$. Then
  $H_1^-=\mathbb Z b$ *is* the quotient $\mathbb Z[b]$ — index **1**. Bloch's
  theorem for the subgroup generator and for the quotient generator is the
  same statement; this is why the conductor-17 computation of Lalín–
  Ramamonjisoa closes with factor 1 with no ambiguity.
- $\Delta<0$ (conductor 11): $c_*: a\mapsto a,\ b\mapsto a-b$. Then
  $H_1^+=\mathbb Z a$, the quotient is $\mathbb Z[b]$, but the anti-invariant
  *subgroup* is generated by $\gamma^-=a-2b\equiv -2[b]$ — index **2**.

Hence, with one fixed factor-1 normalization,

\[
\int_{[b]}\eta(f,g)=D_E\big((f)\diamond(g)\big),
\qquad
\int_{\gamma^-}\eta(f,g)=-2\int_{[b]}\eta(f,g),
\]

and the "factor 2" is not a constant in the theorem but the index of
$\mathbb Z\gamma^-$ in $H_1/H_1^+$. This is stated and proved as
**Lemma (lem:coinvariant)** in §(subsec:anchor), with the full computation of
$c_*$ on the standard basis.

The previously "contradictory" displayed values are now consistent:

- $D_E((x)\diamond(y))=\tfrac52 D_E(P)=\pi b_{11}$ = integral over the
  **quotient** generator $[b]$ (factor 1);
- $\int_{\gamma^-}\eta(x,y)=\pm 2\pi b_{11}$ = integral over the **subgroup**
  generator (index 2);
- and the proof of (C3) uses $[C']=2\gamma^-$, giving
  $\int_{\tilde\gamma}\eta=\frac12\cdot 2\cdot(\pm 2\pi b_{11})=\pm 2\pi b_{11}$,
  i.e. $I_{\mathrm{split}}=b_{11}$ — matching the 366-digit value. Under the
  referee's hypothetical `c=1`-on-$\gamma^-$ reading one would get
  $b_{11}/2$; the resolution is precisely that Bloch's generator is the
  quotient generator $[b]$, not the subgroup generator $\gamma^-$.

## 2. Numerical verification on the conductor-11 curve itself

New script `code/verify_coinvariant.gp` (archive
`notes/attack15-coinvariant.txt`) integrates $\eta(x_W,y_W)$ along cycles
shifted to avoid its poles (PARI `ellztopoint`):

- $\int_a \eta = 0$ (the pairing kills $H_1^+$);
- $\int_b \eta = -\pi b_{11} = D^E\big((x_W)\diamond(y_W)\big)$ — factor **1**
  on the quotient generator (Richardson extrapolation linear in $1/N$,
  matching to $10^{-6}$);
- $\int_{a-2b}\eta = 2\pi b_{11}$ — to $10^{-22}$;
- $-2\int_b\eta=\int_{a-2b}\eta$ to the same accuracy.

So the factor-1 Bloch identity holds verbatim on the conductor-11 curve for
the quotient generator, and the subgroup generator reads exactly twice as
much. (Implementation note, recorded in the archive: unshifted cycles pass
through poles of $\eta$ and give spurious values; all reported numbers use
the shifted cycles.)

## 3. The three-symbol data are now evidence, not anomaly

The three independent tempered symbols of the former "factor-2 phenomenon"
remark (Weierstrass $\{x_W,y_W\}$, our $S_0$-symbol, Bertin's cubic
coordinates $\{X,Y\}$) have $\diamond$-values $-\tfrac52,+\tfrac52,+\tfrac{35}{2}$
times $D_E(P)$ and subgroup-generator integrals $-5,+5,+35$ times $D_E(P)$.
The uniform ratio 2 is now **explained** by the index, and the same data
confirm the index-2 statement of the lemma on three $\mathbb Q$-independent
symbols. The conductor-17 control closes with factor 1 because its index is
1. Remark `rem:diamondk0` has been rewritten accordingly ("resolved"); the
sentence "we have not pursued a mechanism-level proof" is deleted — the
mechanism is the lemma.

## 4. Brunault's footnote 2

The referee is right that footnote 2 only says $\pm\gamma$ are the two
generators. Our reading does not require anything else: Brunault's $\gamma$
is the **subgroup** generator ($H_1^-=\mathbb Z\gamma$), so his (3.211),

\[
\bigl|r_{\gamma}\{x_W,y_W\}\bigr|=\frac{5}{2\pi}D_E(P)=b_{11},
\]

says $\bigl|\int_{\gamma^-}\eta(x_W,y_W)\bigr|=2\pi b_{11}$ — exactly the
index-2 doubling of the quotient value
$\int_{[b]}\eta(x_W,y_W)=D^E((x_W)\diamond(y_W))=-\pi b_{11}$. There is no
contradiction with Bloch's theorem: the two statements concern different
cycles, related by the factor 2 of the lemma.

## 5. The symbol dictionary is no longer load-bearing

Since the proof anchors Bloch's theorem **absolutely** through its proved
conductor-17 instance (Appendix, via the same code and conventions, closed
with factor 1 by the theorem of Lalín–Ramamonjisoa) and then transports the
single normalization to the conductor-11 curve, the Brunault Thm. 118
dictionary step has been **removed from the proof**. It is retained as
Remark `rem:brunault-dict`, explicitly labelled a cross-check, with the
three independent checks of the identification (textual — the "coordonnées
de Weierstrass" pull-back sentence; divisor-wise —
$\operatorname{div}x_W=[A]+[4A]-2[O]$, $\operatorname{div}y_W=2[A]+[3A]-3[O]$,
$(x_W)\diamond(y_W)\equiv 8(O)+5(A)-5(2A)$; numerical — $5D_E(P)/(2\pi)=b_{11}$
to 60 digits). The formerly separate "ratio lemma" (`lem:ratio`,
`eq:ratio`) has been deleted in favour of the direct anchor, so the
"common constant must have one value throughout" requirement is now met by
construction: there is only one statement of Bloch's theorem in the paper,
\[
\int_{\bar\gamma}\eta(f,g)=D_E\big((f)\diamond(g)\big),
\qquad \bar\gamma \text{ generating } H_1/H_1^+ .
\]

Consequently:

- the only non-machine inputs to Theorem (thm:C3) are Bloch's theorem (in
  the Thm. 6 formulation) and Brunault's Corollaire 3.5.101 (3.151)
  $D_E$-values — as now stated at the end of Theorem (thm:cert);
- the appendix status list keeps the factor-1 normalization hypothesis for
  conductor 17, now strengthened: by the index-1 statement of the lemma,
  on a $\Delta>0$ curve subgroup = quotient, so no alternative subgroup
  reading of the factor-1 theorem exists there.
